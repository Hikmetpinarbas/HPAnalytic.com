# HP Football Analysis v0.1
## Evidence chain
SOURCE → NORMALIZATION → EVENT/SEQUENCE → METRIC → MECHANISM → COUNTER-HYPOTHESIS → CLAIM CEILING.
Register important claims with source filename, tournament, game, field, formula, unit, denominator, observation window, interpretation class, and verification status: PASS, REVIEW, BLOCKED, UNKNOWN.
Separate event-level file aggregates from player and team totals; never silently reconcile.

## Pitch and time
Pitch 105×68 meters, x=0…105 and y=0…68 subject to *confirmed source orientation*. User labels longitudinal 1st zone 0–35m, 2nd 36–70m, 3rd 71–105m. For continuous coordinates partition by thresholds 35/70 and document how boundary points are assigned. Time segments 0–15, 16–30, 31–45, 46–60, 61–75, 76–90+ minutes. Timestamp validity and stoppage mapping must be checked separately. Never label spatial meters as elapsed time.

## Prematch
Lead with one falsifiable encounter question, opposing strengths and constraints, historical match context, tactical alternative, measurements that could confirm/refute. Do not use past-match event locations as confirmed positions in an upcoming fixture.

## Postmatch
Start from actual match sequences, game states and adjustments; distinguish process from outcome and opponent response from isolated totals. Compare to prematch forecasts but do not retrofit inevitable explanations.

## Forbidden inferences
No true pressure, speed, acceleration, team shape, player positioning without tracking; no 'guaranteed pressing trap' from low PPDA; no repeatability claim from two matches; no causality inferred from co-located events of different games.

## Deliverables
One claim ledger, one concise central thesis, three to five evidence-backed mechanisms, explicit alternative explanations and a defensible conclusion. If the data do not support this, state the boundary and reduce claims.

# HP Editorial Storytelling v0.1
The Fenerbahçe–Slavia preview source is a *starting structural reference*, not a perfected template or proof of Claude authorship. Source path: `hpfootball-site/src/pages/mac/fenerbahce-slavia-prag-2026-10-20.astro`. Compare with Galatasaray work without importing errors.

## Article architecture
Hero: original thesis + why this encounter matters. Each chapter: football question → observed evidence → mechanism with opponent conditions → counter-hypothesis → what reader learns. Place only relevant graphic within that argument, never a duplicate chart exhibition after the conclusion. End prematch with falsifiable watch points; end postmatch with mechanisms that survived testing and changed meaning.

## Quality bar
Replace generic xG/PPDA recitations with reasoning about play phases, first/second action, spatial access, rest defence, transitions and defensive response *only to the extent supported*. Prefer specific, modest claims over fake precision. No filler paragraph that merely repeats a chart legend. Explain risk where it affects interpretation; long data provenance belongs in compact methodology disclosure.

## Information architecture
Meaningful chapter title, standfirst, selective highlighted evidence, responsive inline figure, and short takeaway. Avoid 2+ consecutive purely explanatory graphics without substantive football analysis. Minimize unnecessary scroll and redundant graphs. Retain established publication date and byline on edits.

## Review
Delete any sentence that could fit any unrelated football match without evidence. Each chart must answer a question that text alone cannot answer better. Mark interpretation distinct from observation.

## Ranked ticker
- Live moving presentation on page + static text equivalent. Pause button, focus pause and prefers-reduced-motion for accessibility.
- Common polarity: positive mint `#76D5B6`, negative coral `#F1A0A4`, letters ivory `#E6EAF0`, numerical values gold `#F8D27B`. Color supplemented by ▲ / ▼ glyph.
- Metric definitions are **directional**. Lower opponent xG and lower goals conceded are positive; PPDA low corresponds to high pressing *intensity*, not always overall team quality.
- Ranks are calculated against 36 SportsBase team summaries. Dataset ties are currently sorted stably rather than tied ranks; refine tie rules before claiming definitive joint ranks.
- Player impact lists require verified player-level tournament inputs and declared definition of positive/negative impact. Do not infer individual performance solely from scoring or from unavailable tracking.
- Gestalt guidelines: similarity (repeated polarity and card layout), proximity (team/metric/value/rank clustered), figure-ground (high-contrast figures), continuity (marquee rhythm), hierarchy (large numbers), and consistent visual feedback. Avoid coercive/subliminal claims; preserve user agency and motion controls.

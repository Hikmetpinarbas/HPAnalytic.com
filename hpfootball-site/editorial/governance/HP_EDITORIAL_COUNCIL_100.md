## Ordinary Reader Test
After one reading, a football-loving reader without analytics vocabulary must be able to answer:
- What is the question?
- What did we actually observe or know?
- What is the tempting but unsafe conclusion?
- What mechanism or distinction changes how I see it?
- What remains unknown?
- What should I look for next time?
Technical terms are allowed only when they buy precision and are inferable or explained in ordinary Turkish.

## Eureka Test
PASS requires PRECONCEPTION, FRICTION, REVEAL, TRANSFER, BOUNDARY and NO TRICK.
Canonical sequence:
SEZGİ → KANIT → ÇATIŞMA → EKSİK PARÇA → MEKANİZMA → KARŞI KANIT → SINIR → YENİDEN GÖRME.
The reveal cannot be withheld merely to manipulate attention.

## Gestalt / cognitive design acceptance
- figure-ground: evidence vs unknown
- proximity: related evidence grouped
- similarity: same semantic role looks related
- continuity: mechanism paths traceable
- closure: incomplete evidence may remain visibly incomplete
- common fate: only genuinely linked temporal/motion relations
- hierarchy/preattentive cues: strongest attention goes to strongest supported idea
- chunking: one cognitive job per section
- progressive disclosure: detail follows comprehension
- dual coding: text and visual reinforce the same bounded claim
- information scent: next question/action is legible
- bounded narrative tension: curiosity never hides a material limitation
